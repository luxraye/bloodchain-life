# Bloodchain Transfuse — Institutional Brief

**Clinical transfusion management** for hospitals and transfusion committees — the ward-facing end of the national blood chain.

## Capabilities (v1)

- Real-time **hospital blood bank inventory** (from released `BloodAsset` units when API connected)
- **Request management** — family emergencies, internal hospital orders, verification workflow
- **Standby donor** coordination for urgent shortages
- **Bedside transfusion** — patient identification, unit scan, ABO compatibility check, audit log
- **Haemovigilance** — structured adverse event reporting aligned with WHO transfusion safety practice

## What Transfuse does not replace

- National laboratory screening (**Mars Lab**)
- Donor collection (**Scyther**)
- Inter-facility logistics (**Voyager**)
- Chronic disease longitudinal registries (**Chronicle**)
- Regulatory returns to MoH (**Sentinel**, via Instances)

## Pilot ask

- One hospital blood bank + transfusion committee sponsor
- Named clinical lead for role provisioning
- Agreement that **unit release** remains Mars Lab workflow
