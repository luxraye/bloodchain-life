# Institutional brief — National Blood Transfusion Service

**Modules:** Scyther (collection) + Voyager (logistics / cold chain)
**Priority:** National conversation — **only after beachhead pilots produce real data**
**Contact link:** https://www.bloodchain.life

---

## Important framing (read first)

NBTS already runs **MEDITECH** for donor tracking and mobile blood bank operations. **We do not pitch displacement.** Bloodchain's role with NBTS is to fill the gaps MEDITECH does not cover — district sites, NGO drives, inter-facility logistics visibility — and to provide a corridor pilot, not a national licence on day one.

This brief should only be sent **after** at least two beachhead pilots (e.g. BLB, a private hospital) are live and generating real numbers. Leading with NBTS before that repeats the original mistake.

## The problem we can help with

- A persistent **17,000–20,000 unit annual deficit**.
- Limited visibility of blood **in transit** between collection, lab, and hospitals.
- District and NGO collection activity that sits outside the central MEDITECH workflow.

## What Scyther + Voyager add alongside MEDITECH

| Capability | Module | Outcome for NBTS |
|------------|--------|------------------|
| District / NGO drive capture | Scyther | Collection data from sites MEDITECH does not reach |
| Dispatch + custody handovers | Voyager | Who held each unit, when, between facilities |
| Cold-chain checkpoints | Voyager | Checkpoint trail along the transport corridor |
| Feed into national KPIs | (High Command) | Once pilots populate real data |

## Pilot proposal

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Scope | One transport corridor (e.g. one collection site -> central lab -> one hospital) |
| Duration | 90 days |
| Success metric | Custody and cold-chain visibility across the corridor that MEDITECH does not currently provide |
| Pricing | Bundled national licence discussion **after** pilot proof |

## What we need from NBTS

1. Agreement that this complements, not replaces, MEDITECH.
2. One corridor and a logistics coordinator to pilot.
3. Clarity on current cold-chain monitoring (sensor-based or checkpoint self-report).

## The ask

Once we can show you real data from independent pilots, a single corridor pilot to demonstrate gap-filling visibility around the existing MEDITECH system.
