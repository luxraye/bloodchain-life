# Institutional brief — Blood for Life Botswana

**Modules:** Scyther (field collection) + Azure (donor portal)
**Priority:** Beachhead #1 — start here
**Contact link:** https://www.bloodchain.life

---

## The problem we are addressing together

Botswana collects roughly 27,000 units of blood a year against a need closer to 45,000 — a shortfall of **17,000–20,000 units annually**. A large share of that gap is a *retention* problem: first-time donors are recruited at a drive and never come back, and there is no shared record that follows a donor from one community drive to the next.

Most community blood drives are still coordinated on paper and spreadsheets. That makes eligibility checks slow, duplicate registrations common, and follow-up with past donors nearly impossible.

## What Scyther + Azure do

| Capability | Module | Outcome for BLB |
|------------|--------|-----------------|
| Donor eligibility screening at the drive | Scyther | Faster, consistent deferral decisions |
| Phlebotomy support and unit labelling | Scyther | Cleaner records, fewer mislabels |
| Mobile-drive setup and roster | Scyther | Run a drive from a tablet, not a clipboard |
| Donor registration and profile | Azure | One identity that persists across drives |
| Donor journey + scheduling + reminders | Azure | Bring lapsed donors back — the retention wedge |
| Trust tiers / recognition | Azure | Lightweight loyalty for repeat donors |

We are pitching **closing part of the 17,000-unit gap through retention**, not a national operating system.

## Pilot proposal

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Scope | One blood drive, end to end, on Scyther + Azure |
| Duration | 90 days (one drive + one retention cycle) |
| Success metric | % of drive-day donors who register on Azure; % contacted for a return slot |
| Pricing | P1,500–3,000 / month operational licence, **or** a per-drive fee for corporate CSR drives |
| Our commitment | On-site setup, staff training, support line during the drive |

## What we need from BLB

1. One named coordinator and one upcoming drive to pilot on.
2. Confirmation of how drives are run today (paper? spreadsheet? any existing software?).
3. Permission to follow up with consenting donors for the retention cycle.

## The ask

A 30-minute conversation and one drive to prove the retention number. If it does not move, we walk away — no national commitment required.
