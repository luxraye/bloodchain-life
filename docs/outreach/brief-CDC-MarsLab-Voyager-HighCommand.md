## Important framing (read first)

This route is about **chain-of-custody and data quality for blood safety** in PEPFAR-supported work, not a government sale. It is most useful as a **credibility and referral** channel (US Embassy / PEPFAR introductions) once we have at least one working pilot. Do not lead the national programme with this brief.

## The problem we can help with

Blood safety programmes depend on verifiable laboratory results and an auditable trail from sample to result to release. Where this is tracked on paper or disconnected spreadsheets, data quality and auditability suffer — which matters directly to funders accountable for outcomes.

## What these modules provide

| Capability | Module | Outcome |
|------------|--------|---------|
| Screening, grouping, component QC, release docs | Mars Lab | Verifiable lab results and release records |
| Dispatch + custody handovers + cold-chain checkpoints | Voyager | Auditable movement of blood/specimens |
| Programme KPIs and audit view | High Command | Funder-grade visibility once data exists |
