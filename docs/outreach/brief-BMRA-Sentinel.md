# Institutional brief — Botswana Medicines Regulatory Authority

**Module:** Sentinel (structured regulatory returns)
**Priority:** Bundle / accelerant — not a standalone BMRA-paid product
**Contact link:** https://www.bloodchain.life

---

## The problem we are addressing together

BMRA is modernising oversight of blood and blood products (MRSA 2013, the AUDA-NEPAD model blood regulations, WHO ML3 maturity goals) while resource-constrained. Today, regulated establishments submit returns as documents and email attachments. There is no structured, verifiable submission channel and no shared review queue — which makes consistent oversight slow and hard to audit.

## What Sentinel does

| Capability | Outcome for BMRA |
|------------|------------------|
| Structured submissions from blood establishments | Returns arrive in a consistent, queryable format |
| Review queue for reviewers | One worklist instead of scattered email threads |
| Document integrity (tamper-evident receipts) | Submissions can be verified, not just trusted |
| Output aligned to the AUDA-NEPAD draft returns | Less manual re-formatting, regulation-ready |

## Governance note (important and deliberate)

A regulator should not buy compliance software from a vendor that also sells to the entities it regulates. We therefore propose:

- **Blood establishments** (the regulated parties) pay for their Sentinel submission tooling.
- **BMRA** receives **free, read-only reviewer access** as a condition of a national agreement.

This keeps the incentives clean and positions Sentinel as a **procurement accelerant** for the wider programme rather than a conflicted regulator sale.

## Pilot proposal

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Scope | One submission type, one or two pilot establishments + BMRA reviewer access |
| Duration | One reporting cycle |
| Success metric | Submissions received and reviewed in Sentinel rather than by email |
| Pricing | Paid by establishments; BMRA access free |

## What we need from BMRA

1. The current required return format(s) and reporting cadence.
2. Confirmation that a structured digital channel is acceptable in principle.
3. One reviewer to trial the queue.

## The ask

Permission to demonstrate a sample return flowing from a blood establishment into a BMRA review queue — no purchase commitment from BMRA.

> **Note:** Confirm BMRA will accept industry-adjacent software and align output to the AUDA-NEPAD draft before investing further. (See `assumptions-log.md`.)
