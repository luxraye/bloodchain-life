## Important framing (read first)

**REDCap is free and entrenched** for research data capture, including at UB. Helix cannot win as "another data-capture tool." It wins only on one defensible claim: **blood-specific custody** — linking research specimens to the actual blood-asset and laboratory records in Bloodchain Core / Mars Lab, with an end-to-end audit trail REDCap does not provide.

Before sending this brief, confirm that the Helix–Mars Lab–Core integration genuinely exists in the build. If it does not yet, this is a roadmap pitch, not a today pitch.

## The problem we can help with

For blood-related studies, specimen provenance and chain of custody matter as much as the survey data. Tracking which physical specimen maps to which participant, where it was processed, and who handled it is exactly where general research tools are weak.

## What Helix adds (only if integrated)

| Capability | Outcome for BHP |
|------------|-----------------|
| Specimen + participant registry | One record linking participant to physical specimen |
| Study audit trail | Custody of each specimen through processing |
| Link to Mars Lab blood-asset data | The blood-specific layer REDCap lacks |
| Role-scoped access (PI, coordinator, ethics read) | Appropriate visibility per role |

## Honest positioning

- **Do not** claim to replace REDCap for general capture.
- **Do** position Helix for **blood/transfusion research** where specimen custody is the requirement.
- Bundle with **Mars Lab + High Command**; sell to institutions already running blood research (BHP, PEPFAR-funded work).

## What we need from BHP

1. Confirmation of whether REDCap is already standard for the relevant studies.
2. One blood-related study where specimen custody is a real pain point.
3. Ethics/data-governance requirements.

## The ask

A conversation to test whether specimen custody is a real gap for a specific blood study — and an honest read on whether REDCap already covers it.
